## Voice Characteristics

### Tone
- **Direct**: Say what you mean. No hedging, no corporate speak.
- **Confident**: We know what we're talking about because we've done it. Not arrogant — experienced.
- **Practical**: Every piece of advice should be actionable. Theory is fine if it leads to a "here's what to do."
- **Honest**: If something is hard, say it's hard. If a project idea won't work, say that. We don't sugarcoat.
- **Peer-to-peer**: We're talking to smart people. Don't talk down. Don't over-explain basics. Do explain things that are genuinely complex.

### What We Sound Like

Good: "Revenue comes first. A token without sustainable revenue mechanics is just a countdown timer."

Good: "We've seen a lot of clever tokenomics. Most of them are dead now."

Good: "When we tell you something won't work, it's because we've seen it fail. Probably more than once."

Good: "Your legal team has what they need for compliance. Your developers have specs for implementation. Your marketing team has content to work with. No one's waiting."

### What We Don't Sound Like

Bad: "Our revolutionary blockchain solutions empower founders to unlock unprecedented value."

Bad: "In the ever-evolving Web3 landscape, tokenomics plays a crucial role..."

Bad: "Simply reach out to our team and we'll get you started on your journey!"

Bad: "Tokenomics.net is proud to announce..."

- Moon / moonshot / to the moon
- Guaranteed returns (or any return promises)
- Revolutionary / disrupting
- Web3 native
- WAGMI / NGMI / crypto slang generally
- Passive income
- Get rich / wealth promises
- Simple or easy (this work isn't, and pretending otherwise is dishonest)
- "Just a token"
- "In the ever-evolving landscape of..."
- "Unlock the potential of..."
- "Leverage the power of..."
- "Game-changing" / "paradigm shift"

## Core Beliefs (Thread Into Content)

1. **Tokenomics should enhance value creation, not replace it.** Most token projects fail because they treat the token as the product instead of as infrastructure.

2. **Revenue comes first.** A token without sustainable revenue mechanics is just a countdown timer. Every model starts with: how does this business actually make money?

3. **Market cap is the constraint.** Theory of Constraints applied to tokens. Features don't matter, yields don't matter, incentive structures don't matter — not if the token can't hold value.

4. **Distribution determines destiny.** You can have the best mechanism design in the world. Poor allocations will kill it. Rushed vesting will kill it. Inadequate liquidity will kill it.

5. **Sustainable beats clever.** We'd rather design something boring that works for five years than something brilliant that implodes in six months.

6. **Compliance matters.** The regulatory environment is getting more serious. Projects that treat compliance as an afterthought are setting themselves up for problems.
